How to read this chart
Gamma Max (GMax)
The strike with highest total gamma × OI. Market makers are long gamma here — they sell rallies and buy dips, acting as a magnet. Price tends to pin at or below GMax.
Delta Neutral (DN)
Where total delta exposure is zero. Below DN, MMs are net long — they buy dips (supportive). Above DN, MMs are net short — they sell rallies (resistive).
Gamma Neutral (GN)
Where total gamma flips sign. Above GN: MMs long gamma (dampening). Below GN: MMs short gamma (amplifying moves). Breakdowns accelerate below GN.
Positive vs Negative bars
Green bars = MMs net long gamma at that strike (dampening). Red bars = MMs net short gamma (amplifying). A wall of green = strong suppression zone.
